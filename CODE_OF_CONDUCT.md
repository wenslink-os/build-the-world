# Community conduct

Be respectful, concrete and patient. Welcome beginners. Critique ideas and code without attacking people. Harassment, threats, discrimination, doxxing and spam are unacceptable.

Maintainers may remove content, pause participation or ban repeated offenders. Decisions should explain the rule involved and allow a private appeal to an uninvolved maintainer where practical. Report concerns privately using the contact channels published by the hosting repository's maintainers. No contact address is assumed in this starter release.
